Life Insurance 101

Life insurance generally pays a benefit to named beneficiaries after the insured person dies, subject to policy terms. Term insurance covers a specified period; permanent policies may include longer-term features and different costs.

Question to ask: Who relies on your income or care?

Emergency Savings

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected necessary expenses. Start with an achievable target and consider keeping funds accessible.

Question to ask: Which surprise expense would be most difficult for your family?

College Planning

Begin with a future education goal, timeframe, and realistic savings amount. Savings options may differ in fees, risks, restrictions, and tax treatment.

Question to ask: What future opportunity do you want to help make possible?

Generational Wealth

Financial literacy, savings habits, family preparedness, and long-term planning can all contribute to building a legacy. Qualified legal and tax professionals can assist with specialized matters.

Question to ask: What financial lesson would you like to pass down?

Educational information only, not individualized financial, investment, insurance, tax, or legal advice.
